Changes since the 5 November review

Decided on 5 November: Option C for mobile navigation, the top announcement bar stays, both EvenGreener Gang sign-ups stay, grid view is the default on collections, and the compare tool takes up to 4 items.

  1. Mobile navigation. Option C is now the default on every page: a compact category row that hides as you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up. Options A and B stay switchable (?nav=a or ?nav=b) for the one-month test.
  2. Header logo. The logo now sits in the true centre of the mobile header, whichever navigation option is on.
  3. Homepage finder. One finder with an explicit first question: Composting, or Water butts and tanks. The next three questions belong to that range, and the water butt questions are the same ones as on the water butt collection page.
  4. Homepage tidy. "Online since 2001" now appears once (the USP strip). Free delivery stays in the announcement bar only, and its USP slot now points to guides and instruction manuals. The bulk strip moved below the finder and is the only bulk block on the page. Bestsellers lead with the Green Johanna and the other high-ticket lines.
  5. Product cards. Cutout image first, lifestyle shot on hover. Products without a cutout yet show their lifestyle shot only (see the Strata Two Bay).
  6. Quick view. Add-ons on the product page and the new "You might also like" row have a small eye button. It opens the item’s description in a panel, so shoppers stay on the page. Same panel pattern as Spread the cost: Esc closes, focus stays inside.
  7. Instruction manuals. New manuals library in the knowledge hub, and an "Instruction manual (PDF)" link under the product details.
  8. Reading times. Removed from the knowledge hub, guide pages and the homepage guides.
  9. Search page. The search field and button are one input group now, the same leaf shape as the newsletter sign-up, so nothing sits out of line.
  10. Order view. Orders show the order date and dispatch date. Parcels sent with a courier that doesn’t track get a plain "This parcel isn’t tracked" notice instead of a Track button.
  11. Bulk enquiries. Sketch of Natalie’s idea: pick products from the bulk range at the top of the page and they are added to the enquiry form.
  12. Sold out. Back in stock state carried into this round: one "Email me when it’s back" button and a "Back soon" link on sold-out cards.

Build tasks after sign-off

Shown in the designs, built in the theme phase.

  • Theme setting for the mobile category row (off / swipe row / compact row), with hotspot tracking on the row for the one-month test.
  • Cart drawer "Goes well with your bag": picked from what is in the basket (rules by product type and collection), not one fixed list of five.
  • Untracked courier notice: a list of untracked carriers in theme settings decides whether the order page shows Track parcel or the notice.
  • Contact form on ElectricForms (fixes blank customer replies and spam), not a mailto.
  • Manual (file) metafield on products, feeding both the product page link and the manuals library.
  • Favourites: a small custom script that saves favourites in the browser and to the customer account, no subscription app. The current site’s wishlist link 404s, so it is replaced.

Build notes. Every moved element is a section or block in Shopify Online Store 2.0, so the merchant can reorder it in the theme editor. Reviews and counts come from Yotpo; the numbers shown are placeholders.

Back in stock. STOQ app block in the product form and on collection cards. Remove the legacy Klaviyo back in stock code and app embed at the theme switch. The STOQ subscriber list lives in the app, so it survives the theme change.

Product facts. Spec values (dimensions, material, origin, recycled content) are placeholders standing in for product metafields. Please confirm them against the supplier sheet.

Accessibility. Tap targets are 44px or more, focus rings use the system ring, and colour pairs follow the system's WCAG 2.2 AA rules. The annotation pins and fold line are review overlays only.
